# ActiveArena demo media

The project website includes the six validated rollout clips and their posters in [`../ActiveArena-website/docs/media.md`](../../ActiveArena-website/docs/media.md). The source samples come from `RoboTwin_Astribot/data_ppt_samples/beat_block_hammer_rotate_view`.

`head` is the policy observation camera. `observer` and `world` are external visualization cameras and must not be used as policy inputs. The website copies are losslessly remuxed H.264 clips with fast-start metadata; `docs/media.json` records dimensions, durations, source/output hashes, and validation details.
